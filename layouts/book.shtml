Tools and Tactics to Scale AI Applications Across the Enterprise

By Ben O’Mahony and Fabian Nonnenmacher

Building a single AI-powered application is relatively simple. Creating a platform that lets many teams build, deploy, and operate agents securely and reliably is the harder and more valuable problem.

This book is a practical roadmap through that entire lifecycle: platform strategy, architecture, model access, runtime, observability, experimentation, governance, and reliability.
